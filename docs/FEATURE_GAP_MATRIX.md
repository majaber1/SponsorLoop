## Unique SponsorLoop Features (No Competitor Has)

1. **Arabic-first bilingual marketplace** — Full RTL support with complete Arabic translations
2. **Saudi city coverage** — 7 cities: Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Khobar, Makkah, Madinah, Tabuk
3. **11-category breadth** — Events, creators, podcasts, sports, digital, OOH, community, gaming, athletes, hackathons, clubs
4. **Explainable AI matching** — 8-component weighted scoring with bilingual reason explanations
5. **Reverse marketplace** — Rights-holders can post their sponsorship needs; brands discover and respond
6. **Trust & Compliance center** — Organization verification queue, trust scores, compliance matrix
7. **Proposal versioning** — Track proposal iterations within the Deal Room
8. **Combined marketplace + intelligence** — Discovery, deal management, analytics, and market insights in one platform
