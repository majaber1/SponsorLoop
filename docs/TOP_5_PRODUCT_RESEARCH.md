# Top 5 Sponsorship Marketplace Competitor Research

Research date: August 2026

## Executive Summary

This document captures the publicly available product capabilities of five leading sponsorship platforms. Each serves a different niche: SponsorUnited is a data/intelligence platform (not a transactional marketplace), OpenSponsorship is a two-sided athlete-brand marketplace, SponsorPitch is a sponsorship research and prospecting tool, Sponsorium is enterprise portfolio management software, and Sponsoo is a European sports sponsorship marketplace. SponsorLoop's positioning as a Saudi-first bilingual marketplace has no direct competitor in this set.

---

## 1. SponsorUnited

**Website:** https://sponsorunited.com  
**Founded:** 2018 (Stamford, CT)  
**Category:** Sponsorship Intelligence Platform (SaaS)  
**Latest version:** SponsorUnited 4.0 (launched February 2026)

### Value Proposition
"The Leading Sponsorship Intelligence Platform." Positions itself as a unified operating system for planning, evaluating, and activating sponsorship partnerships using the world's largest sponsorship dataset combined with purpose-built AI.

### Data Scale
- 403,000+ brands tracked
- 2.2 million deals documented
- 21.1 million data points across sports, entertainment, media, and talent
- $13.3 billion in actual sponsorship deal transaction data

### Feature Capability Matrix

| Dimension | Capability | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| **Landing page / value prop** | Intelligence-first positioning; "plan, evaluate, activate" | Targets brands, rights holders, agencies, media partners |
| **Brand onboarding** | Enterprise sales-led; demo request flow | No self-serve signup publicly visible |
| **Rights-holder onboarding** | Enterprise sales-led; separate solutions page for rights holders | Tailored intelligence for rights holders to benchmark assets and build pitches |
| **Marketplace discovery** | Not a transactional marketplace; discovery is research-oriented | Filter by brand, property, category, geography, deal attributes |
| **Opportunity profiles** | In-depth company sponsorship profiles with deal history | Shows brand activations, assets, partner networks |
| **Audience / reach data** | White-space audience analysis; audience overlap intelligence | Helps identify untapped audience segments |
| **Campaign brief creation** | Not a primary function | Platform focused on intelligence, not campaign execution |
| **Matching / recommendations** | AI-powered partner recommendations; white-space opportunity identification | Surface AI provides real-time intelligence for decision-making |
| **Comparison features** | Competitive benchmarking; deal comparison across properties | Pricing benchmarks against market data |
| **Sponsorship packages** | Not applicable (intelligence, not transaction) | Provides pricing context and benchmarks for existing deals |
| **Proposal creation** | Proposal Evaluator: AI-driven analysis of inbound proposals | Benchmarks, pricing context, negotiation guidance |
| **Negotiation / deal pipeline** | CRM designed for sponsorship professionals | Tracks opportunities, activations, renewals |
| **Messaging** | Not publicly documented as a feature | CRM may include internal collaboration tools |
| **Deliverables / activation** | Activation planning and tracking within CRM | Asset-by-asset deal tracking |
| **Campaign measurement / ROI** | ROI metrics tracked within CRM; market-level benchmarks | SPND methodology for sponsorship valuation |
| **Dashboards** | Comprehensive analytics dashboards | Deal trends, market activity, competitive intelligence |
| **Pricing model** | Custom enterprise pricing; no free trial | Not publicly disclosed; estimated $20K-100K+/year based on enterprise SaaS positioning |

## 2. OpenSponsorship

**Website:** https://opensponsorship.com  
**Founded:** 2015 (New York, NY)  
**Backed by:** Serena Williams, David Blitzer  
**Category:** Two-Sided Athlete/Creator Marketing Marketplace

### Value Proposition
"The World's Largest & Smartest Marketplace in Sports Sponsorship." Connects brands with 25,000+ professional athletes, influencers, podcasters, and creators across 160 sports and 120 countries.

### Feature Capability Matrix

| Dimension | Capability | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| **Landing page / value prop** | Marketplace-first; "find athletes, run campaigns, measure results" | Emphasizes database size (25K+ athletes) and full-service option |
| **Brand onboarding** | Self-serve free tier for browsing; paid plans for full access | Plans: Free browse, $79-$1,250/mo self-serve, $2,000/mo Full-Service, $5,000/mo ELITE |
| **Rights-holder onboarding** | Free registration for athletes at all levels | Athletes/agents set their own pricing directly on profiles |
| **Marketplace discovery** | Search by sport, gender, location, follower count, engagement rate | 150+ sports, 40+ countries, filtering across multiple dimensions |
| **Opportunity profiles** | Athlete profiles with audience demographics, engagement rates, past campaign results, pricing | Lifestyle information, popularity, current sponsorships, recent news |
| **Audience / reach data** | Social media audience demographics, engagement rates | Third-party verified data for post performance |
| **Campaign brief creation** | Comprehensive creative briefs aligned with brand voice | Outlines key messaging, "Do's and Don'ts," managed approvals |
| **Matching / recommendations** | AI-based matching; proprietary search technology | Provides shortlists of 3X needed athletes; iterative refinement |
| **Comparison features** | Multi-athlete comparison dashboard | Side-by-side evaluation of demographics, engagement, pricing |
| **Sponsorship packages** | User-generated pricing on athlete profiles | Social media posts, event appearances, product placement, logo usage |
| **Proposal creation** | Direct proposal sending through platform | Brands can send proposals and communicate directly |
| **Negotiation / deal pipeline** | In-platform negotiation of terms | Both sides review and finalize terms before deal confirmation |
| **Messaging** | Unified messaging interface for brand-athlete communication | Biweekly calls and strategy sessions available on managed plans |
| **Deliverables / activation** | Platform manages approvals, content review | Full-service plan oversees execution from brief to delivery |
| **Campaign measurement / ROI** | Dashboard analytics + third-party verification | Reach, engagement, site actions, campaign-specific KPIs |
| **Dashboards** | Campaign performance dashboard; athlete comparison dashboard | Centralized view of active campaigns and results |
| **Pricing model** | Hybrid: brand subscription ($79-$5,000/mo) + 10-20% commission on athlete deals | Free for athletes; no commission on product-only deals |

**Category:** Sponsorship Research, Prospecting & Sales Tool

### Value Proposition
"Real-time online recommendation engine for sponsorship." Uses predictive intelligence and analytics to help 70,000+ members find the right partners faster. Bridges the gap between sponsoring brands and properties.

### Data Scale
- 13,000+ sponsors/brands tracked
- Real-time sponsorship deal database
- 70,000+ platform members

### Feature Capability Matrix

| Dimension | Capability | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| **Landing page / value prop** | Research-first; "search, discover, connect" | Targets both sponsors and properties (rights holders) |
| **Brand onboarding** | Self-serve membership starting at $59/month | Tiered plans with increasing data access |
| **Rights-holder onboarding** | Properties can create profiles and showcase pitches | Properties share information about events/opportunities |
| **Marketplace discovery** | Intuitive filters across 13,000+ sponsor profiles | Filter by category, location, sponsorship activity, deal size |
| **Opportunity profiles** | In-depth company sponsorship profiles with deal history | Shows current partnerships, key decision-maker contacts |
| **Audience / reach data** | Sponsor demographic analytics; audience insights | Limited compared to social-first platforms |
| **Campaign brief creation** | Pitch showcase for properties | Properties can share pitch materials; not a full brief builder |
| **Matching / recommendations** | AI-powered recommendations; Customized Lead Alerts | Alerts by company, category, location; correlation-based recommendations |
| **Comparison features** | Competitive benchmarking of sponsorship deals | Analyze competitor sponsorship activity and trends |
| **Sponsorship packages** | Not a primary feature | Pitch marketplace allows properties to present packages |
| **Proposal creation** | Pitch tools for properties to reach sponsors | Contact unlock and outreach management |
| **Negotiation / deal pipeline** | Built-in CRM for prospect tracking | Save prospects, track outreach, organize pipeline |
| **Messaging** | Contact unlock; external outreach (not in-platform messaging) | Finds decision-maker contact info for direct outreach |
| **Deliverables / activation** | Not a feature | Research/prospecting tool, not deal execution |
| **Campaign measurement / ROI** | Deal trend analytics; market-level insights | Not campaign-level ROI |
| **Dashboards** | Sponsorship activity analytics; CRM dashboard | Deal trends, market activity, alert management |
| **Pricing model** | SaaS subscription starting at $59/month | Tiered by data access depth; no transaction fees |

## Key Takeaways for SponsorLoop

1. **No competitor serves the Saudi/MENA market or offers Arabic language support.** This is a clear whitespace opportunity.

2. **Full-stack marketplace + intelligence is rare.** OpenSponsorship and Sponsoo are transactional marketplaces but lack deep intelligence. SponsorUnited has deep intelligence but is not transactional. Combining both is a differentiation opportunity.

3. **Escrow/payment integration is a trust differentiator.** Only OpenSponsorship and Sponsoo handle money. In the Saudi market, local payment methods (mada, STC Pay, SADAD) would be essential.

4. **AI matching is table stakes.** All platforms now offer some form of algorithmic or AI-powered matching. SponsorLoop's explainable matching is a differentiator.

5. **Campaign brief creation is underserved.** Only OpenSponsorship offers structured campaign briefs. Most platforms leave this to offline processes.

6. **ROI measurement varies widely.** From none (Sponsoo) to market benchmarks (SponsorUnited) to campaign-level (OpenSponsorship) to portfolio-level ROO (Sponsorium). A unified approach would stand out.

7. **Rights-holder onboarding is key to supply-side growth.** Sponsoo's <1 minute signup and OpenSponsorship's free athlete registration set the bar.
