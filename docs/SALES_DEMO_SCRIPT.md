# SponsorLoop 10-Minute Sales Demo Script

**Audience:** Potential investors, enterprise clients, or strategic partners
**Setup:** Browser at `sponsorloop.sa` (or localhost:3000 for dev). Start on Arabic landing page.

---

## Minute 0-1: The Problem (Arabic Landing Page)

> "The Saudi sponsorship market is worth over 4 billion riyals, but there is no marketplace connecting brands with rights-holders transparently. Deals happen through personal connections, pricing is opaque, and there is no way to compare opportunities or track ROI."

**Action:** Show the Arabic landing page. Point out the hero section: "Every sponsorship opportunity in one place."

> "SponsorLoop is Saudi Arabia's first bilingual sponsorship marketplace — built Arabic-first."

**Action:** Switch to English (`/en`) to show bilingual capability.

---

## Minute 1-3: The Marketplace

**Action:** Navigate to Marketplace (`/en/marketplace`).

> "Over 20 vetted sponsorship opportunities across 11 categories — events, creators, podcasts, sports, gaming, universities, and more — in 7 Saudi cities."

**Action:** Demo the filters:
1. Click "Events" category tab → show filtered results
2. Select "Riyadh" city filter → further narrowed
3. Change sort to "Highest trust" → reorder
4. Toggle to list view → compact comparison
5. Click the heart icon on a card → "Brands can save favorites for later"

> "Every listing shows pricing, estimated reach, and a trust score — before any conversation starts. No more guesswork."

**Action:** Click into an opportunity detail page (e.g., "Riyadh Tech Summit").

> "Each opportunity has audience breakdown, ROI calculator, deliverables, and similar opportunities — everything a brand needs to make a decision."

---

## Minute 3-5: AI-Powered Matching

**Action:** Navigate to AI Planner (`/en/planner`).

> "Our AI planner uses an 8-component matching algorithm to find the best opportunities for any brand's specific needs."

**Action:** Set budget to 100,000 SAR, select target audiences, click "Find matches."

> "Each recommendation comes with a match score and full breakdown — audience fit, budget alignment, reach potential, trust score, timing, category relevance, geographic match, and past performance. This is explainable AI — brands see exactly why each opportunity is recommended."

**Action:** Expand the match breakdown on the top result.

---

## Minute 5-7: Deal Room and Pipeline

**Action:** Navigate to Deals list (`/en/deals`), then click into a deal.

> "Once a brand finds the right opportunity, they enter our Deal Room — a structured 7-stage pipeline from initial request through delivery and completion."

**Action:** Show the Deal Room layout:
1. Point out the stage indicator (e.g., "Negotiation")
2. Show the Proposal Builder → "Versioned proposals so both sides can iterate transparently"
3. Show the Deliverables Tracker → "Real-time progress tracking with completion percentages"
4. Show the Messages panel → "Built-in communication — everything in one place"

> "No more scattered WhatsApp threads and lost email attachments. Every proposal version, every deliverable, every message — tracked and auditable."

---

## Minute 7-8: Rights-Holder and Admin Views

**Action:** Sign out, sign in as Owner (rights-holder).

> "Rights-holders — event organizers, content creators, sports teams — get their own dashboard."

**Action:** Show the Owner Dashboard.

> "They see incoming sponsorship requests, their listed opportunities, active deals, and revenue metrics."

**Action:** Sign out, sign in as Admin. Navigate to Admin panel.

> "Platform administrators have a verification queue to vet every listing, KPI dashboards, and full pipeline visibility."

**Action:** Show the pending verification items and click "Approve" on one.

---

## Minute 8-9: Analytics, Compliance, and Trust

**Action:** Sign in as Brand. Navigate to Analytics (`/en/analytics`).

> "Full analytics dashboard — spend tracking, ROI metrics, campaign performance across all deals."

**Action:** Navigate to Compliance (`/en/compliance`).

> "Saudi-specific compliance center with regulatory guidelines and brand safety scoring. We take trust seriously — every listing is verified with a trust score out of 100."

**Action:** Navigate to Compare (`/en/compare`).

> "Side-by-side comparison tool for evaluating multiple opportunities simultaneously."

---

## Minute 9-10: Business Model and Close

**Action:** Navigate to Pricing (`/en/pricing`).

> "Three tiers: Free for discovery, Pro for active deal-making, Enterprise for agencies managing multiple brands. Revenue model: SaaS subscription plus transaction fees on closed deals."

> "We are building the infrastructure layer for Saudi Arabia's sponsorship economy. The market is fragmented, the timing is perfect with Vision 2030, and we are the first platform built specifically for this region — Arabic-first, compliant with Saudi regulations, and designed for how business actually works here."

> "Let me show you one more thing..."

**Action:** Switch to Arabic (`/ar`) and quickly navigate through marketplace and planner.

> "Every feature works natively in Arabic with full RTL support. This is not a translated product — it is built for this market from the ground up."

**Close:**

> "We are looking for [investment / partnership / pilot clients]. Let us discuss how SponsorLoop can [transform your sponsorship strategy / fit into your portfolio / serve your organization]."

---

## Key Talking Points (if questions arise)

- **Market size:** Saudi sponsorship and advertising market estimated at 4+ billion SAR
- **Vision 2030 alignment:** Entertainment, sports, and events sectors growing rapidly
- **Competitive advantage:** Only Arabic-first platform; explainable AI matching; end-to-end deal management
- **Technology:** Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript — modern, fast, scalable
- **Data model:** Supports PostgreSQL for production with enterprise-grade security
- **Expansion:** GCC markets (UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman) as Phase 2
